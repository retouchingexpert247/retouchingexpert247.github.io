Ghost Mannequin Photo Editing

Create clean and professional invisible mannequin images without showing the mannequin or model.

Our Ghost Mannequin Photo Editing Service helps clothing brands, apparel manufacturers, fashion photographers and eCommerce businesses create professional product images.

We carefully remove the visible mannequin and combine the required sections of the garment to create a natural three-dimensional appearance.

Our editors pay close attention to clothing shape, seams, collars, sleeves, folds, shadows and fabric texture so the final image looks clean and realistic.
